# KINDUPP Québec Business Setup Checklist

This is an administrative research checklist, not legal or tax advice. No
business registration, NEQ request, tax registration, payment or government
submission was made.

## Decisions and checks

- [ ] Confirm the legal operator and whether `KINDUPP` will be used as an
      enterprise/assumed name by a sole proprietor.
- [ ] Check Québec name availability, French-language name rules and the exact
      registration requirement for the chosen name with the Registraire des
      entreprises du Québec (REQ) or qualified counsel.
- [ ] Confirm whether registration is required and the deadline for this
      operator. Québec's official page says an individual operating under a
      name other than their full first and last name generally must register
      within 60 days after starting activities; verify applicability and
      current rules for the actual facts.
- [ ] If registration is required, decide what business address will be
      public; the REQ filing may make required address information public.
- [ ] Determine whether an NEQ is required and gather identity/business details
      only after confirming the filing path.
- [ ] Ask a Québec accountant whether the app's revenue model, future fees,
      subscriptions or platform commissions are taxable supplies and how
      marketplace/independent-helper treatment applies.
- [ ] Monitor worldwide taxable supplies of the operator and associates. The
      general GST/QST small-supplier threshold is $30,000 over a calendar
      quarter and the four preceding calendar quarters, with exceptions and
      specific registration timing. Confirm exact application with Revenu
      Québec/CRA before charging tax or registering voluntarily.
- [ ] Confirm income-tax reporting for sole-proprietor business income and
      recordkeeping with an accountant.
- [ ] Update public legal identity/contact fields only after the owner makes
      these choices; do not publish a home address without informed approval.
