## Description

Kindupp connects you with people who have already lived what you are going
through.

Moving to a new country, starting a new job, dealing with a big life change:
someone has been there before. On Kindupp you can:

- Ask the community and read answers from people with real experience
- Send private messages
- Arrange free voice or video calls
- Block and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable

Members share their personal experience. Kindupp is not legal, immigration,
financial or medical advice.

Kindupp is for adults 18 and over.

## App Review notes

Kindupp is a free community app. Paid features are disabled in this version.

Sign in with Apple or email.

To test calls, open the menu on a post in the home feed and choose audio or
video call. Users can block
and report from any profile, post or chat. Account deletion: Profile >
Settings > Delete account.
