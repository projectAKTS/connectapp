# French Legal Texts: Drafts For Review

Prepared by Claude on 2026-10-31 from `lib/config/legal_texts.dart`.

**Status: drafts only.** They are not used by the app or the website. The
English texts stay the published versions until the founder approves the
French ones after review.

- **Review:** a qualified French-speaking reviewer (ideally the lawyer
  consulted for Québec) must check them. They are a careful translation,
  not legal advice.
- **Charter of the French Language, s.55:** standard-form contracts (the
  Terms of Use) are expected to be offered in French first in Québec.
- **Marked passages:** `[À CONFIRMER]` marks text that is **not** in the
  English version. It is suggested for accuracy or Québec Law 25 and needs
  a decision:
  1. **Usage statistics:** the app now collects them only if the user
     turns them on (I18N-1). The English Privacy Policy still says they
     are collected; it should be updated too, after approval.
  2. **Person in charge of personal information:** Law 25 requires
     publishing this person's title and contact. By default, this is the
     founder.
  3. **Data outside Québec:** Law 25 frames transfers outside Québec. A
     privacy impact assessment is required before them.
  4. **Minimum age:** the English Terms say 18. Keep 18, or allow 14+?
- **Terminology:** "aidant" is used for "helper". In Québec, "proche
  aidant" also means a family caregiver. The reviewer may prefer another
  word, such as "mentor" or "accompagnateur".
