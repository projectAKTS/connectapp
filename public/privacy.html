Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Kindupp collects, why, who receives it, and your rights. It is written for Canada's PIPEDA and Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

Who is responsible

Kindupp is operated by Yakup Akturk, sole proprietor, Québec, Canada.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the Founder, support@kindupp.ca

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address, sign-in method (email, Google or Apple).
  • Profile: photo, bio, city, country, time zone, the languages you speak, topics you need help with, the experience you can share, availability.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, messages and attachments (photos, videos), and reviews you give after a session.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, call type and status. The audio and video of calls are not recorded or stored by Kindupp.
  • Sessions: free sessions you book and their status. Payments are turned off in this version, and Kindupp does not collect payment card information.
  • Age declaration: your confirmation that you are 18 or older, with its date. We do not collect your birthdate or ID.
  • Safety: reports you file or that concern you, and who you blocked.
  • Device: push notification tokens and a random installation id, used to deliver messages and calls to your device; your app language, used to write notifications in it.
  • Crash reports (optional, off by default): if you turn on "Send crash reports", technical details of crashes (device model, OS and app version, error and stack trace). Not linked to your account.
  • Usage statistics (optional, off by default): if you turn on "Share usage statistics", which screens and features are used. Not linked to your account and never includes your messages or posts. No advertising identifier is collected.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

Why we use it

  • To run Kindupp: accounts, profiles, matching people by topic and language, posts, messages, calls and sessions.
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, reports, blocking, preventing abuse and fraud.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • If you allow it: to fix crashes and improve the app.

Who receives it (service providers)

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Hosting): accounts, database, files, server logic, notifications, abuse protection and the website. Stored in the United States.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics: crash reports and usage statistics, only if you turn them on. United States.
  • Apple (push notifications, incoming calls, Sign in with Apple) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Sign-In): notifications and sign-in. Device tokens and sign-in identity. United States.
  • Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch: automated safety check of post images and a frame from post videos. Chat media is not sent.
  • Agora: real-time audio and video calls. Call media passes through Agora's network, which may use servers outside Canada. Calls are not recorded.

Other users see your public profile, your posts and comments, and the messages you send them.

Transfers outside Québec and Canada

Our providers process and store information outside Québec, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. We choose providers whose terms require them to protect it.

How long we keep it

  • Account deletion: when you delete your account in the app, we delete your sign-in account, profile, posts and the photos and videos you uploaded. If part of the deletion fails, our servers retry it automatically.
  • Kept after deletion: messages and chat media you sent stay in the other person's conversation. Review stars stay in a helper's average without your name; review comments are removed. Your comments on other people's posts are deleted (or emptied, if others replied under them), and your name is removed from call records. Reports and call invitation records may be kept to protect the community.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: kept by Google Firebase for a limited time under its retention settings.

Optional data: how we ask

After sign-up we ask once, separately, whether you want to share crash reports and usage statistics. Both are off unless you turn them on, and saying no changes nothing else. You can change either at any time in Settings.

Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your profile, change the app language, turn crash reports and usage statistics on or off, block people, and delete your account, all in the app.
  • Ask to access or correct your personal information, withdraw consent, or ask how it is handled: support@kindupp.ca. We answer within 30 days.
  • You may complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Adults only

Kindupp is for people aged 18 and over. We rely on your declaration and do not check ID. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we suspend it.

Security incidents

We keep a register of confidentiality incidents. When an incident presents a risk of serious injury, we notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the people affected, as the law requires.

Changes

We will tell you in the app about important changes to this policy.
