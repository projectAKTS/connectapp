Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Helperly collects and how we use it, in line with Canada's PIPEDA.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, sign-in provider.
  • Profile: photo, bio, country, languages, topics and expertise you add.
  • Content: posts, comments, messages and attachments you share.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, and for how long. We don't record the audio or video of calls.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We store a payment reference, never your full card number.
  • Device and usage: push notification tokens, app version, crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and app usage events such as screens viewed (Firebase Analytics), which help us fix problems and improve the app. They don't include your messages or posts.

How we use it

To run the service (profiles, matching, messages, calls, payments), to keep the community safe (moderation, reports, blocking), to send notifications you've enabled, and to fix bugs and improve the app.

Who we share it with

Service providers who run parts of Helperly for us: Google Firebase (hosting, database, sign-in, notifications, crash reports, analytics), Agora (calls) and Stripe (payments). Other users see your public profile and the content you post. We don't sell personal information.

Where it is stored

Our providers may store data outside Canada, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, posts, photos and personal data, at once or at most within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example, payment records). Messages you already sent stay in the other person's conversation.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile, change notification settings, and delete your account in Settings. You can ask us for access to or correction of your personal information at support@helperly.app.
