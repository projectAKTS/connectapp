Delete your Helperly account

In the app

Open Profile, tap the settings icon, then Delete account. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@helperly.app from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, posts and comments
  • Your sign-in account and notification settings

What we keep

  • Payment records we must keep by law
  • Messages you already sent, which stay in the other person's conversation
