Delete your KINDUPP account

In the app

Open Settings and choose Delete account. The app removes the sign-in account and most profile data; some records may remain as described in the Privacy Policy draft.

Without the app

Email support@kindupp.ca from the address you used to sign up. We will follow up to verify and process the request; contact support for current status.

Data removed through the app flow

  • Your sign-in account, profile, posts, and related user media
  • Some profile references, comments, and review text

Records that can remain

  • Messages/media in another participant's conversation, safety reports, and call records
  • Consultation/payment records and data retained by providers or backups
