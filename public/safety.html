If you are in danger

  • Emergency: call 911.
  • Suicide or mental health crisis: call or text 988 (Canada, 24/7).
  • Under 20: Kids Help Phone, call 1-800-668-6868 or text 686868.

Helperly is a peer community. Helpers share their own experience. They are not lawyers, licensed immigration consultants, doctors or counsellors, and nothing on Helperly is legal, immigration, medical or financial advice.

Stay safe

  • Keep payments inside Helperly. Never send money outside the app.
  • Do not share passwords, banking details or ID documents.
  • For immigration applications, use a licensed consultant (RCIC) or a lawyer.
  • Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable. Use the ... menu on a post, profile or chat. Reports are private.
  • Block people you don't want to hear from.

Questions? Contact us at support@helperly.app.

Community guidelines

Helperly works when people feel safe asking for help.

  • Be kind and respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or bullying.
  • Share experience, not professional advice. Say what worked for you. Don't present yourself as a lawyer, consultant or doctor unless you are verified as one.
  • Be honest. No fake profiles, scams, spam or misleading claims.
  • Respect privacy. Don't share other people's personal information.
  • Keep it legal and safe. No illegal content, sexual content, or content that puts anyone at risk.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules.
